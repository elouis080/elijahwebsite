Eli's Portfolio

Audio Engineer • Web Designer • Creative Technologist

Elijah Louis is a New York–based audio engineer turned web designer. I began audio engineering on Logic Pro X, which eventually led me to earn an A.A.S. in Audio Engineering from LaGuardia CC. I gained professional studio knowledge, DAW expertise, and a deep understanding of how to bring an artist’s vision to life.

After spending years in the underground music scene, I’ve released original music, performed at a range of venues, and collaborated with artists whose ideas I helped shape into polished mixes. My goal has always been to create spaces—both physically and digitally—where creativity can thrive.
