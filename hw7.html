HW 7: Illustrated Environment

For my illustrated environment, I chose a simple image so I could focus on tools and shortcuts in Adobe Illustrator. I had fun outlining the subject and exploring color variations to give the illustration depth. Now that I've integrated it into the website, I wish I had focused more on contrast. Though readability can be encouraged by changing site contrast (which is something I learned building this site), I'd have preferred the photo stand out on its own. This was a great learning opportunity.
