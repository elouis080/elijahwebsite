HW 7: Illustrated Environment

For my illustrated environment, I chose a very simple image so that I could focus more on the tools and shortcuts in the Adobe Illustrator software. If I were more versed in the application, I would have added darker shades of green to the horizon to emulate the trees. I may still attempt this out of curiosity; I think it would look really cool. Although I wanted to use something simple, I didn't want something boring, which is why I chose an image with a subject in it. I didn't want to just outline the mountains and the ground...that felt like a cop-out. So, I had some fun with outlining the subject in the image. The color restriction really allowed me to see how I could play with different shades of the same color to get more depth out of my illustration. Again, had I the skills, I would have played with shading on the hiker's outline as well. Despite my technical shortcomings, I think the final product came out great.
