HW 4: Photomontage

Part 1: Land of the Free

My first image explores social commentary on the state of our country and the foundations on which it was built. The flag is distorted, blood spatter added for dramatic effect, and the image is rotated to simulate a distress signal.

Part 2: Royal Predecessors

My second image highlights Black pioneers of music inside an Elvis outline. Figures include James Brown, Chuck Berry, Little Richard, and Sister Rosetta Tharpe. This collage addresses appropriation of Black music in history while emphasizing the contributions of these musicians.
